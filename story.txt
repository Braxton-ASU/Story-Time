The day of the potluck had finally arrived.
I was a bit anxious with my last minute food choice;
I was going to bring apfelkuchen, but I had ended up deciding to bring broetchen because
then I could flaunt my homemade sauerkraut with it.

Once I arrived, a hund burst through the door with a doener in its mouth, and I could hear shouting inside,
and then I went in.

As I reached for a broetchen, a big glob of sauerkraut slid right onto my brand-new Dirndl and
Just as I was scraping sauerkraut off my Dirndl,
someone cranked up Rammstein on the speakers, and we all ate our broetchen headbanging to “Du hast.”

As we were headbanging my Handschune slipped off my hands and ended up being blown away by a heavy wind.

The wind was so strong that it carried my Handschuhe all the way down the Hauptstrasse,
past a parked Porsche and the Baeckerei,
until a Pantoffelheld on his Drahtesel caught them,
while "Du hast" played on in my head like an Ohrwurm.
